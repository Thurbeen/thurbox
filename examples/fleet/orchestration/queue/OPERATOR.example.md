# Operator instructions

Copy this form to private storage and supply its path in worker briefs.
It records standing preferences, publication constraints and approval rules;
it contains no credentials and grants no authority beyond each task's brief.

- State the repositories and outcomes the worker may change.
- Name required gates and independent review obligations.
- State whether publication, merge or deployment is authorized.
- Name genuinely external decisions and the origin channel for questions.
- Require workers to follow POLICY.md and finish with `fleet result`.
- Keep private machine details and operational records out of public artifacts.
